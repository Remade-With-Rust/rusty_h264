THE FIVE "VETO GATES" ARE THREE DECISIONS
================================================================================
aq_grain_veto, sub8_grain and mbtree_grain all call the SAME predicate,
`FrameSignals::grain_signature()`:

    has_ref && median_var < 200 && grain_floor > 5.0 && mgain < 0.1

They cannot diverge; they share thresholds. The census lists them as three gates,
which is why grain_akiyo reads 96.7% / 100% / 100% across them (the 96.7 is
frame-vs-MB granularity, not a different decision). A refit of the conjunction
moves all three at once.
