The earlier census concluded "four of five veto gates never fire". THAT WAS
WRONG, and the cause was the CORPUS, not the gates: it was run on six clips
containing NO grain clip and NO screen clip, while aq_grain_veto, sub8_grain
and mbtree_grain all key on GRAIN. Asking a grain veto about non-grain content
and recording 0% is not evidence of a broken gate.

gatecheck ships a DEFAULT clip list that is deliberately one-per-gate-relevant
class (grain_akiyo, screen_text, harbour_4cif, foreman_cif, akiyo_cif) and it
was overridden. `video-tests/synth_clips.sh` generates the two synthetic ones;
they were simply absent from the working tree.

WITH THE GRAIN CLASS RESTORED, THE GATES WORK:

  aq_grain_veto    96.7% on grain_akiyo,  0.0% everywhere else
  sub8_grain      100.0% of 11,484 on grain_akiyo,  0.0% elsewhere
  mbtree_grain    100.0% on grain_akiyo,  0.0% elsewhere
  intra_rd_flip    19.1% of 11,484 on grain_akiyo, NEVER RUN elsewhere
                   (it is grain-gated by design -- correct behaviour)
  sub8_split/      NEVER RUN on grain_akiyo, because sub8_grain suppressed
  sub8_rd_revert   100% of them. The veto CHAIN works.

That is textbook content-adaptive dispatch: fires on the class it was built
for, silent off it.
