VERDICT — and the limit on what this can claim
============================================================================================
Of 63 (clip x gate x structure) cells, exactly ONE moved by more than 2 points:
akiyo_cif `sub8_rd_revert`, 52.6% -> 48.3% on n=477. Everything else is within
+/-1.2 points, and the all-intra half is identical to the decimal.

PROOF-OF-ARM, and why bytes are the WRONG proof here. Total coded bytes moved only
-0.01% / +0.02% between the arms, which looks like an arm that never engaged. It is
not: a BD-rate improvement at FIXED QP shows up as some mix of fewer bits AND better
quality, and gatecheck records only bytes, so it structurally under-reads the change.
The real evidence the arm is live is that the gate DENOMINATORS moved -- the encoder
visited different macroblock counts:

    FourPeople   sub8_split  22236 -> 22128    sub8_rd_revert 4097 -> 4025
    akiyo_cif    sub8_split   2268 ->  2244    sub8_rd_revert  477 ->  462
    harbour_4cif sub8_split 148632 -> 148524   sub8_rd_revert 35324 -> 35311

SO THE HONEST SCOPE IS THREE CLIPS, NOT SIX. On grain_akiyo, mobile_cif and
screen_text every denominator is IDENTICAL between the arms -- 8x8 changed nothing
the gates could see, so those rows carry no information about the question and must
not be counted as evidence that the thresholds survived. Reporting them as six
passing clips would be the untouched-signal mistake: a signal that did not move
localises nothing until you have shown it COULD have moved.

What this does support: on the three clips where 8x8 demonstrably changed the encode,
the gates keep firing at essentially the rate they were fitted at. R6b's strong form
-- "8x8 invalidates every gate threshold" -- is not supported by this evidence.

What it does NOT support: that the thresholds are correctly CALIBRATED under 8x8. A
gate can fire at an unchanged rate and still be firing on the wrong macroblocks; fire
rate is not accuracy. The per-clip BD half of R6b is what would answer that, and the
one cell that did move (akiyo sub8_rd_revert) is on the clip whose stream changed
most -- the coherent place to look first.

TOOL CHANGES made to run this at all:
  * gatecheck's coding structure was HARD-CODED to gop 30, so no census had ever
    covered all-intra -- the structure where 8x8 actually bites (-1.90% BD on akiyo
    vs +0.12..0.34% in I+P). Now RUSTY_GATECHECK_GOP / _BFRAMES / _QP.
  * RUSTY_GATECHECK_T8 PINS the arm. With 8x8 default-on, an arm that merely omits
    the flag is the ON arm, and the A/B would have compared a setting against itself.
