== WHY screen_text DID NOT MOVE: the loss is FLAG OVERHEAD, not bad picks ==
With the veto firing, a screen encode still WRITES transform_size_8x8_flag as 0
on every eligible MB. Against --transform-8x8 0 (flag absent from the PPS):
  screen_text  qp22 -0.14%  qp27 +0.19%  qp32 +0.73%  qp37 +0.39%
  screen_ui    qp22 +0.01%  qp27 +0.06%  qp32 +0.14%  qp37 +0.26%
The gap GROWS with QP — a fixed per-MB cost against a shrinking budget. That is
the flag-overhead signature. A frame-level veto cannot remove it; only omitting
transform_8x8_mode_flag from the PPS can, which is a SEQUENCE-level decision.
